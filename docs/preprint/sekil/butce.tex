\node[altyazi, text width=128mm]
     at ($(current axis.south west)+(-17mm,-14mm)$)
  {\textbf{What the model does not know.} Every term is measured by a
   controlled pair of our own runs, not borrowed from the literature.
   Open circles are measured and then kept \emph{out} of the denominator
   on purpose --- an upper bound, and a branch this scene does not take ---
   since admitting either would make the test easier to pass. The
   near-field term is quoted as $0.053$ in the pool protocol but is still
   \texttt{nan} in the code: a real gap, recorded rather than filled in.};
