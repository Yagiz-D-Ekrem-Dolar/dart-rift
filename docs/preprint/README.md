# DART-RIFT. I. --- pilot-results preprint

[Read the four-page PDF](../../output/pdf/DART-RIFT_Pilot_Results_2026-10-10.pdf).

The vector figures are also available separately: [four-observable pilot atlas](../../output/pdf/Figure_1_Four_Observable_Atlas.pdf), [locked production design](../../output/pdf/Figure_2_Locked_Design.pdf), and [impact-site / joint-response comparison](../../output/pdf/Figure_3_Pilot_Sensitivity.pdf).

This preprint reports five completed, validity-checked simulations: a three-point cohesion sweep, one matrix-tension toggle, and one 25 m impact-site offset. Its vector figures are generated from the checked pilot values and the registered 96-point coarse / 12-point fine design. The paper distinguishes these scene-conditioned findings from a production posterior or a sealed numerical Hera forecast; neither is reported here. This is Paper I of a two-paper series. Paper II will carry the production posterior and the numerical Hera predictions if the registered claim gates pass. They are planned as two separate archival records rather than two versions of one, since Paper II reports exactly what Paper I declines to report. Neither identifier has been assigned or deposited.
